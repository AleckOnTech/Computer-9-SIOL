Vision

A Relevant, Transformative Catholic School Community in Joyful Mission with Home and Parish

Mission

We are wholeheartedly committed to a Culture of Excellence in and through:
  1. Formation of Families including all Sectors of the School Community,
  2. Relevance of Curriculum, Programs, and Services Attuned with the 21st Century Education,
  3. Promotion with the Integrity of Creation,
  4. Enhancement of Parish Involvement,
  5. Service to the Poor,
  6. Deeper Appreciation and Witnessing of the Philippine Catholic School Standards
  7. Collaboration towards Viability of Resources, and
  8. Empowerment of all School Personnel towards greater Professionalism.
